Overview

Built for the moment someone forgets.

Elderly family members, patients on multi-drug schedules, and busy households all share the same problem — remembering to take the right medicine at the right time. SehatSaathi turns that into a routine the device handles for you.

Reminds on time

A buzzer and LED activate at each scheduled dose time, so the reminder is heard and seen — even from another room.

Dispenses the dose

The correct compartment releases into the tray, which slides out to the front — so the right medicine is ready to pick up.

Built to extend

The core cycle — schedule, alert, dispense — leaves room to add caregiver alerts, lockouts, and tracking as the project grows.
